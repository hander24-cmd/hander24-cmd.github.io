CURRENT RESEARCH

Pitch Selection, Performance & Pitcher Health

University of Maryland Baseball

Researching how pitch selection and pitch characteristics relate to long-term pitcher health while maintaining effectiveness on the mound.

The project examines factors associated with pitching injuries, including velocity, spin rate, pitch type, and pitch usage. The goal is to better understand which combinations of pitches may reduce stress on pitchers while still producing effective results.

Research Question: What pitch combinations provide the best balance between pitcher health and performance?

IN DEVELOPMENT

Developing a Volleyball Passing Metric

Maryland Volleyball

Developing a metric designed to quantify the quality of a pass and make passing performance easier to evaluate.

The goal is to move beyond basic outcomes and create a consistent measure that helps coaches and players understand how effectively a pass puts the team in position to run its offense.

Goal: Create an interpretable metric that can identify areas for improvement and provide clearer feedback on passing performance during matches.

COMPLETED

Baseball Scouting Visualization Application

Loyola Marymount University Baseball

Developed an application to help LMU Baseball coaches and staff visualize scouting data and make analytical information easier to interpret during the scouting process.

The application was designed around the needs of the coaching staff, transforming scouting information into a more accessible visual format that could support game preparation and player evaluation.

The application uses private team scouting information, so the underlying data, application, and specific outputs cannot be displayed publicly.
